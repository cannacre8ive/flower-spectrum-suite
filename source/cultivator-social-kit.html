The two devices

Everything in this kit is built to be recognized without a logo. Two elements do that work, and they should appear on nearly every asset.

The Flower Spectrum strip is the proportional band. The loudest aroma profile leads, left to right, and each segment’s width is its share of the signal. A profile only joins the strip if it scores at least 60% of the leader; three colors is the ceiling. A solid band means one aroma dominates — a split band is an honest blend.

The fingerprint ID is the ten-sector radial. Every profile owns a fixed wedge, so the same shape reads the same way on any flower — a visual signature you can spot across a whole feed. It shows the full ten-profile distribution behind the strip.

Make it yours

Set your farm name and handle up top and every template updates live. To feature your own flower, run its COA through the Chemovar Classifier — the strip, fingerprint, blend name, and confidence all come straight from the engine, so nothing here is ever hand-drawn or invented.

Compliance — non-negotiable

These are aroma classifications, not effect or medical claims. Aroma leads on every asset; THC % is factual data, not a claim. Keep “sedative / energizing / entourage” language off anything public. The compliance line rides on every template on purpose — keep it there.

A note on these three strains

Run through the canonical engine, the real Ideal Cannabis panels classify by their loudest measured aroma: Jelly Breath reads Earth-Spicy, while Sunday Driver and Kush Mints both read Citrus-Floral. That’s the system being honest — it weights how a terpene actually smells, treats Gas as an emergent balance rather than a single note, and only ever classifies what the COA supports.
