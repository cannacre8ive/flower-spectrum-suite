01 / THE DESIGN PROBLEM

A consistent identity across
different kinds of work.

A retail menu, a teaching sheet, and a social post serve different audiences. They still need to tell a coherent story.

DISCOVER

Retail experience

A buyer searches the local flower catalog by aroma and specific terpene concentration, compares fingerprints, and sends a shortlist to a print menu.

UNDERSTAND

Classifier + education

A farm uploads a PDF, CSV, or scanned terpene report and flower photo. Editable source rows, unit checks, total reconciliation, and human review precede classification.

EXPRESS

Labels + creative

The reviewed flower generates social posts, a chemovar sales card, and six-up labels with a clean preview. Every output carries the fingerprint and band.

The integration decision

Preserve the original source and working behavior. Extract shared profile identities, sample panels, and classification math. Add a clear suite navigation, connected handoffs, local persistence, and a presentation layer that makes the system easy to explore.

Sources: digital menu, education, social, classifier, print menu, labels; wholesale portal preserved as reference.

Evidence and boundaries

This is a portfolio demonstration, with illustrative commercial data and historical sample panels supplied in the prototypes. New reports are processed locally and require source review. Historical samples retain their original provenance; no independent scientific review is implied. Educational copy needs editorial review before retail use. No live POS connection, production access control, or measured business uplift is claimed.
